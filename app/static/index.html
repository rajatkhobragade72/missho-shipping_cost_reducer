Meesho Shipping Optimizer

Hero image ke kai versions banao, Meesho par cost check karo, sabse sasti wali use karo, phir baaki photos usi style me banao.

Step 1: Hero image upload karo

Tip: sirf ek product ki saaf photo do. "Buy 1 Get 1" jaise banner, text, ya ek se zyada pieces wali photo se Meesho product ko bada/bhaari samajh sakta hai.

Step 2: Meesho par cost check karo

  1. Automatic (aasaan): Chrome extension (folder extension) lagao. Ye is tool ke bina khud chalta hai: Meesho Supplier Panel par "Meesho Shipping Optimizer" panel me "1. Hero image ki shipping kam karo" dabao, wo khud variations bana ke test karega aur sabse sasti laga dega.
  2. Extension jo asli shipping number padhta hai, wo is page par khud-ba-khud aa jata hai (har 4 second me refresh). Har photo par "Meesho par test hui" tag lag jayega, sabse sasti photo par "USE THIS".
  3. Manual: Variations download karo aur Meesho Supplier Panel par catalogue me upload karke shipping charge dekho (submit mat karna).
  4. Jo cost manually pata chala wo card ke "Meesho cost" box me likho. Sabse sasti image #1 par aa jayegi aur uspar "USE THIS" likha aayega.
  5. "Comparison sheet" se ek image milegi jisme har variation par number aur cost likha hoga (sirf aapke dekhne ke liye, Meesho par upload mat karna).

AI hero images (Pollinations): upload ke baad apne aap 12 style banenge

AI band hai. .env.example ko .env me copy karo, usme POLLINATIONS_API_KEY (enter.pollinations.ai/keys se) daalo aur server restart karo.

Aapki photo Pollinations ke server par bheji jayegi. AI kabhi-kabhi product badal deta hai, upload se pehle dhyan se check karna. "Clean white studio" wali image se 8 extra framed versions bhi bante hain (alag size/zoom). Har baar dobara chalane par naye results aate hain.

Step 3: Baaki catalogue images

Chuna hua hero:

Dusre angles ki photos upload karo (max 10). Sab usi size aur zoom me banengi taaki catalogue ek jaisa dikhe. Plain/white background wali photos sabse achhi aati hain.
